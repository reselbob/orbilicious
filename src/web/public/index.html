What Is the Opening Range Breakout (ORB)?

ORB tracks the first 15 minutes of the New York session to define an opening high/low range, then looks for a confirmed breakout beyond that range before entering a trade.

In this app, trades are risk-sized, target a configured reward multiple, and are monitored until stop, target, or end-of-day handling conditions are reached.

Liquidity Zone Finder
Liquidity Zone Finder v1 scans the most active symbols for the selected session date and ranks likely liquidity zones.

In stock trading, liquidity is the concentration of buy and sell interest sitting around important prices. Those areas often form near prior highs, prior lows, breakout levels, gaps, and zones where many traders are likely to place stops or trigger entries.

Finding liquidity zones matters because price often accelerates when it moves into them. Traders use these zones to anticipate where momentum may expand, where reversals may begin, and where large participants may be active. That makes liquidity mapping useful for timing entries, setting stops, and choosing realistic profit targets.

This Under Construction feature will scan the most active stock symbols and surface the price areas where liquidity is likely clustered so ORBilicious can evaluate them before and during the trading session.
