What Is the Opening Range Breakout (ORB)?

ORB tracks the first 15 minutes of the New York session to define an opening high/low range, then looks for a confirmed breakout beyond that range before entering a trade.

In this app, trades are risk-sized, target a configured reward multiple, and are monitored until stop, target, or end-of-day handling conditions are reached.
